## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an earthy, commanding, and authoritative mineral intelligence platform engineered for geological exploration teams, mine operations managers, and autonomous field engineers. The aesthetic bridges the raw physical realities of mining extraction—deep sub-surface strata, weathered sandstone, and oxidized copper veins—with the precision and rigor of AI-driven telemetry.

The design movement is **Tactile Technical Minimalist**:
- **Earthy Grounding:** Replaces cold, generic enterprise blues and artificial dark-mode neons with deep, rich mineral charcoal (`#1E1915`, `#2C1A14`) paired against sandstone and aged parchment tones (`#F7F3EE`, `#EDE6DD`).
- **Precision Metallics & Telemetry:** Copper and terracotta accents serve as active energy indicators, simulating raw core samples and copper concentrates.
- **Architectural Utility:** Crisp, high-density data layouts, structured bento cards, tactile controls, and high-visibility status vectors create a decisive command environment where high-risk operational decisions are executed with total clarity.

## Colors

The palette derives its tonal depth from mineral deposits, geological core extraction, and open-pit topography. 

- **Primary (`#9E5A2A`) & Tertiary (`#C87D55`):** Calibrated terracotta and raw copper. Used for primary operational calls to action, active map targets, drill review triggers, and prioritized alert markers.
- **Secondary (`#2B231D`) & Deep Neutrals (`#1E1915`):** Rich earth and sub-surface charcoal. Used for primary typography, structural framing, high-emphasis KPI figures, and navigation containers.
- **Backgrounds (`#F7F3EE`, `#EDE6DD`, `#E3DACF`):** Sandstone and warm parchment layers. These provide a low-glare surface suited for high-density tabular ledgers, GIS data layers, and prolonged control-room shifts.
- **Telemetry Indicators:**
  - `telemetry-emerald` (`#2D6A4F`): Nominal production, model confidence, auto-resolved ledgers.
  - `telemetry-amber` (`#C47C17`): Moderate risk, fleet throttling, maintenance warnings.
  - `telemetry-crimson` (`#A63A2A`): Critical shortfall, route bottlenecks, safety thresholds.

## Typography

Typography balances technical instrumentation with human legibility under field and command center conditions:

- **Headlines & Telemetry Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its structural geometric proportions and crisp technical character. All data indices, coordinate readings, tonnage metrics (P50), status tags, and navigation headers leverage its legible numeric glyphs and industrial profile.
- **Body & Narrative Context (`Work Sans`):** Selected for high micro-reading clarity within data tables, geological prospectivity summaries, and AI reasoning drawers. It retains neutral clarity across high-density layouts.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Tabular numbers (`tnum`) must be enforced for all production forecasts, sensor values, coordinate grids, and ledger listings to prevent layout shifts during live polling.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a disciplined, fluid 12-column modular grid built on a strict 4px/8px baseline grid rhythm:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Sidebar accommodates a 68px collapsed state expanding to 224px. The Command Center, Scenario Builder, and Production Forecast use a multi-pane bento structure (e.g., 3-column control/projection/evidence splits).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Panels collapse into stacked accordion tabs or bottom pull-up sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Sidebar transitions to a dedicated slide-in drawer anchored from the left. Dynamic telemetry bars adapt to horizontal swipe stacks or direct compact stat cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through tactile surface stacking and crisp structural borders rather than heavy blur-based drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Bedrock Canvas):** Sandstone `#F7F3EE`. The foundational canvas across all routes.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Warm Parchment `#EDE6DD` bound by a 1px solid `#D8CFC4` border. No shadow is applied; division is sharp and architectonic.
- **Level 2 (Active Panels, Flyouts & Modals):** Elevated Parchment `#E3DACF` supported by a grounding low-spread shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 25, 21, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Drawers & Critical Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#EDE6DD` framed with an outer 1px `#B46A36` accent or deep `#2C1A14` border with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 25, 21, 0.16)`.
- **Mineral Sheen:** Select geological map pins, active AI drill targets, and attention badges use a subtle, localized metallic glow: `0 0 12px rgba(180, 106, 54, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is calibrated to `roundedness: 1` (Soft), projecting industrial strength, tool-grade precision, and geological structure:

- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Selectors, Chips):** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius. Rejects bubbly or playful radii in favor of chiselled utilitarian profiles.
- **Cards, Bento Modules, Drawers:** 8px (`0.5rem`) outer corner radius.
- **Nested Inner Containers (Ledger Rows, Telemetry Blocks):** 4px (`0.25rem`).
- **Telemetry Indicators & Badges:** Slightly pill-softened or crisp rectangular badges with 2px to 4px radius, preserving data density and clear baseline alignment.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Controls
- **Primary CTA ("Find Feasible Response", "Simulate Scenario", "Send for Approval"):** Solid Terracotta (`#9E5A2A`) with parchment text (`#F7F3EE`), 4px border radius, 1px tone-on-tone border (`#864B20`). On hover, transitions to `#864B20` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary Actions ("View Baseline", "Export", "Refresh"):** Parchment surface (`#EDE6DD`) with earth charcoal text (`#1E1915`) and a 1px solid border (`#D8CFC4`). Active state darkens to `#E3DACF`.
- **Destructive/Critical Action:** Bordered or solid `#A63A2A` with pure white text.

### Attention Cards & Risk Vectors (InsightCard)
- **Structure:** Structured bento card using `#EDE6DD` background and a 3px left-border anchor keyed to severity:
  - Critical: `#A63A2A`
  - Warning: `#C47C17`
  - Nominal: `#2D6A4F`
- **Content Flow:** Upper row displays the uppercase micro-badge (`Space Grotesk`, `label-sm`), followed by an earth-charcoal summary title (`headline-sm`), an impact description (`body-sm`), and a bottom action strip with a discrete directional trigger.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Natural Language & Parameter Inputs:** Warm sandstone field (`#F7F3EE`) encased in 1px border (`#D8CFC4`). Focus state activates a 1px ring in copper accent (`#9E5A2A`) with zero ambient shadow drift. Monospaced placeholder text in `#766F68`.

### Sliders & Discrete Scenario Toggles
- **Track:** 6px structural track filled with `#D8CFC4`. Active filled span uses `#9E5A2A`.
- **Thumb:** 18px square or bevelled circular handle in `#1E1915` with a 2px inner copper ring (`#C87D55`).
- **Discrete Multi-segment Selectors:** Enclosed rectangular segmented pill in `#EDE6DD`. The selected toggle snaps to `#1E1915` with cream text (`#F7F3EE`).

### Data Tables & Prediction Ledgers
- **Header:** Sticky top, uppercase technical labels (`label-sm`), background `#E3DACF`, border-bottom 2px `#D8CFC4`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#EDE6DD` to `#F7F3EE`). Cell padding `12px 16px`. Active simulation executing rows pulse with a gentle border highlight (`#9E5A2A`).

### Modals & Drawers
- **Response Plan & Evidence Drawers:** Slide-in sheet from the right edge with a solid `#EDE6DD` background, top metallic branding strip, and high-contrast section headers delineating machine telemetry and confidence intervals.
